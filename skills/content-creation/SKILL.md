# Content Creation

## Ground truth
- Every product claim comes from the live site or the brand's docs. Never invent products, sizes, features, prices, or promos.
- Keep the brand's voice (e.g. "we" as the brand). A founder voice only when the owner asks.
- Link to and riff on existing pages (guides, landing pages). Don't make up new facts.
- Keep a short rules file (what we sell, what we don't, voice, formats) and re-read it every run.

## Plan
- Make a calendar (e.g. 2-4 weeks) with date, format, visual style, tone, and topic per post.
- Test one variable at a time (tone, visual, or format) against a matching pair. Read results after about 72 h as a hint, not proof.
- Tie topics to the season and to what's selling.

## Draft as issues
- One issue per post: date + format in the title. The body has the caption, hashtags, asset path or preview, and link.
- Label `content`, `social:draft`. The owner approves by switching it to `social:approved`.

## Formats
- Use each platform's native sizes (e.g. feed 4:5, reels/stories 9:16). Crop with the subject centered; never stretch.
- No prices or offers baked into images. They go stale.
- Lifestyle props in a photo must not read as products you sell.

## Publish
- On a schedule, publish or schedule only `social:approved` issues. Then swap the label to `social:published` (or `social:scheduled`) and comment the post URL.
- Login, 2FA, or a paid boost needed: label `social:blocked`, stop, and ask the owner. Never enter passwords; work in a session the owner signed into.
- Nothing approved: stay quiet.

## Comments and DMs
- Draft replies for approval. Answer only from known facts. Send order or support issues to the support channel.
- Bugs or complaints you see: file them with `write-spec`.

## Never
- Post, boost, or spend money without approval.
- Confuse the brand with similarly named accounts. Don't engage with them.
- Share customer data or private details.

## Report
- Tell the lead what was published (URLs) and early numbers (likes, saves, comments, shares) so it lands in `shipped-digest`.
